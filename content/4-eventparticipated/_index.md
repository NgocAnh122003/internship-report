Throughout the internship journey at the FCAJ Workforce Bootcamp, alongside intensive research and technical project implementation, I actively participated in all offline meetings, technical discussions, and collaborative study sessions with the program community.

Below is a summary of the activities attended:

### 4.1. Offline Technical Discussions & Community Meetups with FCAJ

- **Activity Name**: FCAJ Offline Technical and Cloud/AI Collaborative Learning Series.
- **Time & Location**: Throughout the internship period at the program's venue.
- **Role**: Active member, engaging in group discussions, focused learning, and collaborating with peers to solve hands-on AWS challenges.
- **Main Content**:
  - Updating modern Cloud-Native technology trends.
  - Discussing system architecture methodologies and troubleshooting techniques for VPC, Serverless, and CI/CD pipelines.
  - Exchanging practical experiences among participants and mentors.
- **Lessons Learned & Personal Contributions**:
  - Gained valuable systemic thinking perspectives through peer collaboration.
  - Enhanced teamwork spirit and built a quality professional network with fellow cloud enthusiasts.
